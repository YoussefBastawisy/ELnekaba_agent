# Nile Systems — Staff Handbook (extract)

## Section 1 — Annual leave
Full-time staff receive 21 working days of paid annual leave per calendar year.
Public holidays are additional and are never deducted from this balance.
Part-time staff receive a pro-rata entitlement based on contracted days.

## Section 2 — Carry over
Up to 5 unused annual leave days may be carried into the following year.
Carried days must be used before 31 March or they are forfeited.
Carry over above 5 days requires written approval from the Head of People.

## Section 3 — Booking leave
Leave must be requested at least 14 days in advance and approved by a line manager.
Requests inside 14 days require HR approval in addition to the line manager.
Leave cannot be booked during a declared month-end close period.

## Section 4 — Expenses
Meals during travel are reimbursed up to 400 EGP per day under expense code EX-114.
Receipts must be submitted within 30 days of the expense being incurred.
Expenses submitted after 60 days are not reimbursed under any circumstances.

## Section 5 — Refund approvals
Customer refunds under 3000 EGP are approved by a team lead.
Refunds of 3000 EGP or above require Finance sign-off before being issued.
No refund may be issued without a documented reason code.
